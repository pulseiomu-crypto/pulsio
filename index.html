The platform

One island. One map. Everything happening on it.

Every day the island moves — weather shifts, power cuts roll through districts, swells decide which lagoons are safe, cyclones form a thousand kilometres out. That information exists, scattered across a dozen websites and WhatsApp forwards. PulsIO gathers all of it onto a single live map of Mauritius, refreshed around the clock, readable in one glance.

The feed

What the island tells you.

WX
Live weather
Temperature, wind, humidity and UV across ten stations island-wide, refreshed every ten minutes.
CYC
Cyclone tracking
Every system in the Indian Ocean — position, class, winds and trajectory — the moment MMS publishes a bulletin. Always free.
CEB
Power cut alerts
Live and scheduled outages by district, down to the affected streets and the estimated restoration time.
CWA
Water disruptions
Low pressure, bursts and maintenance across the network, with fix estimates as they're announced.
SEA
Beach & lagoon conditions
Sea state, tide, jellyfish reports and crowd levels — so you pick the right beach before leaving home.
FLT
Flights & marine traffic
Every aircraft over the island and every vessel around it, moving live on the map.
NWS
News & events
Breaking headlines from the island's newsrooms, tonight's events, and the week's fuel prices.
RPT
Community reports
Accidents, floods, hazards — reported by people on the ground and verified before they reach the map.
